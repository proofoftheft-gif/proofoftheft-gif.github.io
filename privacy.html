Privacy policy

"Proof of Theft Buzz" · effective 4 November 2026

Who this covers

"Proof of Theft Buzz" (the app) is a private tool used only by the owner of the Proof of Theft YouTube channel to manage that one channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

  • Through the YouTube Data API: the Proof of Theft channel's own videos and their details (title, description, tags, category, privacy status, publish schedule, thumbnails, playlists).
  • Through the YouTube Analytics API: the Proof of Theft channel's own analytics (for example views, watch time and subscribers), read-only.

The app works only with the Proof of Theft channel. It checks the channel ID before every change and refuses to act on any other channel.

How the data is used

  • Analytics are read to produce private progress reports for the channel owner.
  • Video details are changed only after the channel owner approves each individual change.
  • The data is not used for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Storage, sharing and security

Access tokens and any data the app reads are stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in a private folder readable only by the owner. Nothing is stored on a server run by the app. No data is sold, rented, shared with or transferred to anyone.

Google API Services

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The app uses YouTube API Services. See the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account's third-party access page: myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access stops the app immediately. Locally stored tokens can then be deleted by the owner.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the effective date changed. Questions: proofoftheft@gmail.com
